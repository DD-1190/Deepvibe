Process

A Proven & Streamlined Design Process: Research, Build, Iterate, Scale

Our process creates seamless digital experiences that drive adoption, boost retention, and fuel growth—while ensuring usability, scalability, and brand consistency through research-driven, iterative design.

Research & Validation

Goals: Understand product, users & business feasibility

  • Understand product vision & business goals
  • Conduct user research
  • Benchmark competitors & industry trends
  • Validate market demand & technical feasibility

Strategy & Concept Development

Goals: Structure the experience & validate early ideas

  • Develop User Personas / JTBD
  • Map User Journey & Information Architecture
  • Create low-fidelity wireframes & concepts
  • Conduct early validation (Stakeholder/User feedback)

Design & Prototyping

Goals: Build & test realistic designs

  • Design high-fidelity UI with a component library
  • Create interactive prototypes with animations
  • Address edge cases & accessibility issues before final testing
  • Conduct concept testing before full-scale user testing

Testing, Handoff & Iteration

Goals: Validate, refine, and ship

  • Conduct user testing & gather insights
  • Iterate based on feedback before finalizing
  • Design handoff with dev specs & assets
  • Establish Design System for future scalability
